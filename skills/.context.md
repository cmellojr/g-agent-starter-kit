## Constraints

- Skill files MUST follow the schema defined in skills/README.md.
- Skill filenames MUST be lowercase and hyphenated.
- Persona-specific skills MUST be prefixed with the targeted persona name.
- Procedures MUST be procedural; declarative constraints belong in rules/.
